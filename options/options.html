Xpeaker

Reads X / Twitter posts aloud through chrome.tts + Supertonic voices — no host server.

Per-author voices (override auto)

Word highlighting

Highlights the current word as it's read. Needs the voice engine to emit word events; if it doesn't, the caption still shows the full text.

Focus Mode (full-screen reader)

The animated backdrop behind each post. Turn on the Mood ring (below) to colour whichever scene you pick by the post's emotion. Switches live while Focus is open.

Focus Mode is the full-screen reader — the Xpeaker mark on the bar, the popup, or the right-click menu. With sound on, clips within the limit above play with audio (the first time the browser blocks it, a one-time "enable sound" prompt appears). Clips longer than the limit show as silent background while the caption is read. With sound off, all videos are silent background.

Mood ring (experimental · on-device)

Runs a small emotion classifier entirely in your browser — no text ever leaves your machine. Enabling it downloads a one-time ~83 MB model (then cached). Currently surfaces in Focus Mode.

Keyboard shortcuts

All shortcuts are Alt + the key. The bar's ‹ button expands to show the active shortcuts on-page too.

Changes save automatically.
