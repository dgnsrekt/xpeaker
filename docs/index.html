Xpeaker

Read your timeline out loud.

A Chrome extension that reads X / Twitter posts aloud, right in your timeline. Click the ▶ read button on any post, or drop into a full-screen Focus reader. Everything runs on your device — no account, no servers, no tracking.

  • ✓ Single or continuous "thread" reading that auto-scrolls and skips ads
  • ✓ Focus Mode — a full-screen reader, one post at a time over a live animated background
  • ✓ Per-author voices, or a distinct voice auto-assigned per author
  • ✓ Karaoke word-highlight captions; reads full "Show more" posts
  • ✓ Optional on-device mood ring tints the background to each post's emotion
  • ✓ Ticker shader modes — mention a $cashtag and the background reacts to its price move
  • ✓ Keyboard shortcuts — Default or Vim-ish
